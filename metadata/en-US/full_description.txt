Pocket creates a separate Android work profile for copies of your apps. Each copy has its own accounts and app data. By default, copied apps use your configured proxy; optional routing settings can send selected traffic directly.

Features:

- Copy installed apps into Pocket, install APK files, launch copies and create home screen shortcuts.
- Import connection links, QR codes, files and subscriptions. Supported connections include Hysteria 2 and VLESS with REALITY over TCP.
- Choose a connection for an individual app.
- Use Android's Always-on VPN and lockdown controls inside the work profile.
- Transfer connections, subscriptions and settings in a password-protected backup.

Pocket requires Android 10 or newer, a device with Work Profile support, and your own compatible proxy server or subscription. Compatibility and network protection depend on the device, Android implementation and configuration. Pocket is under active development; not every hardware and failure scenario has completed testing.
